# Pricing

The kit is free (MIT). The paid tiers are for teams that want it running on their projects without doing the work.

| Tier | Price | What you get | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup** | **$600** fixed | Installed on up to 3 projects (Windows, macOS or Linux), the two jobs scheduled, `NOW.md` and `CORRECTIONS.md` seeded from your last two weeks of transcripts, one 30-minute review call, 14 days of fixes. | 3 business days |
| **Customization** | **$2,500** fixed | Everything in Setup, plus your own brief sections (your tracker, ticket system or docs), your session-naming conventions, a corrections lexicon tuned to how your lead actually gives feedback, delivered as a fork with both-ways self-tests. | 2 weeks |
| **Multi-machine** | **$6,500** fixed | Everything in Customization for a team: cross-machine memory export/import through your git repo, lane watchdogs for unattended agents, usage pacing against your subscription meter, a shared corrections ledger. | 4 weeks |

All tiers: fixed price, half on start and half on acceptance, acceptance defined in writing before work starts, source
delivered with tests. No retainers. Invoices are issued through PayPal; details are sent with the proposal.

**What is not sold:** advisory or consulting by the hour, anything involving your confidential data beyond the transcripts
on your own machines (which never leave them), and anything that needs a model call to work.

Contact: the address in the repo profile.
